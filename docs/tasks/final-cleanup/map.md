## What this map is

A captured holding pen for stragglers: landed-but-unwired surfaces,
vacuous mechanisms, deferred removals, and open product considerations
that earlier efforts consciously postponed. This map is NOT charted:
no tasks exist and the frontier is empty by design. Its time comes
after the initial set of efforts (the phase sequence in plan.md and
any adopted post-v1 maps); the entry grilling (wayfinder) then
re-checks every straggler against the codebase as it stands and
decides ship, fix, or remove for each.

## How stragglers land here

trigger (the condition that would make the deferred question real).
